Our Core Principle

"YOUR BUSINESS DATA. YOUR GOOGLE ACCOUNT. YOUR CONTROL." All your invoicing, customer, quotation, and expense records are stored directly inside a spreadsheet in your personal Google Drive — not our servers.

1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate using Google OAuth 2.0, we collect basic profile details provided by Google — including your email address, profile name, avatar image URL, and Google user ID — to establish your authenticated session.

2. Google Drive & Google Sheets Permissions

We request access to Google Sheets and Google Drive (drive.file scope) solely to create and manage your business database spreadsheet named Business Billing Data. We do not inspect, read, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.

3. Data Ownership & Storage

All customers, products, quotations, invoices, payments, and expense ledgers are written directly to your Google Spreadsheet. We do not sell or monetize your business records in any way.

4. Account Deletion & Right to Erase

You can permanently delete your account at any time from the Settings page. Deleting your account immediately triggers a Google Drive API deletion of your Business Billing Data spreadsheet and erases all local session tokens.

5. Contact Us

For any privacy questions or concerns regarding Google OAuth permissions, contact the platform developer at vsgrpsemail@gmail.com.
